Stitches for Care

We turn crochet into community impact—designing and handmaking comfort items that are donated to people facing hardship. Through consistent service, we bring warmth, dignity, and connection—one stitch at a time.

Our Mission

Stitches for Care is a student-led initiative that transforms creativity into measurable service. We create handmade crochet items—blankets, hats, scarfs, and comfort pieces—and partner with community organizations to deliver them to individuals in need. Our goal is to build a culture of empathy and leadership by mobilizing volunteers to serve through making.

what we do

  • Create: produce high-quality handmade comfort items.
  • Donate: coordinate delivery with trusted community partners.
  • Engage: welcome volunteers to learn, contribute, and lead.

How to get involved

  • Volunteer: help crochet items, sort supplies, or assist at events—no experience required.
  • Donate Supplies: contribute yarn, hooks, stuffing, or finished items to support our projects.
  • Partner With Us: we work with hospitals, and community orgs to deliver items to people in need.
