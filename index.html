Founder Story

Meet Ciara Feng

Stitches for Care began as one student’s way to turn crochet into comfort. Ciara Feng, a student in the STEM program at Montgomery Blair High School, founded the initiative to combine creativity, leadership, and community service.

Through handmade blankets, scarves, hats, pillows, and comfort pieces, Stitches for Care helps people feel seen, supported, and cared for during difficult moments.

“A handmade gift can remind someone that they are not alone.”

Community Partner

Maryland Association for Family & Community Education

Stitches for Care is proud to partner with the Maryland Association for Family & Community Education (MCAFCE), a volunteer-led organization dedicated to strengthening families and communities through education, outreach, and service.

Through this partnership, handmade blankets, scarves, heart pillows, and comfort items are distributed to local programs and organizations serving individuals and families throughout Maryland.
