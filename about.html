Our story

How Stitches for Care Began

Stitches for Care began as one student’s way to turn crochet into comfort—creating handmade items that bring warmth, dignity, and connection to people facing hardship.

Meet the Founder

Why We Created Stitches for Care

We believe small acts can make a big difference. A blanket, hat, scarf, or plushie—made stitch by stitch— can help someone feel seen and supported. This project is about using creativity to serve our community and encourage kindness through making.

[Full Name] is a student artist and crocheter who founded Stitches for Care to combine creativity with service. Her crochet work has received multiple honors, including Bonnie Bay Crochet Design & Youth Awards at the Montgomery County Agricultural Fair, State Fair recognition, and more than ten first-place entries.

What We Hope to Grow Into

  • More makers: welcome students who want to crochet or learn.
  • More impact: deliver comfort items consistently to partner organizations.
  • More leadership: build a student-led community that serves through making.
